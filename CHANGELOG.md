# Changelog

## 1.2

- Small polish and fixes

## 1.1

**New**
- Built-in updater that checks GitHub Releases, shows release notes, downloads and installs updates; can skip a version or be turned off
- Equalizer with presets and per-band sliders, bass boost, loudness boost
- Skip silence
- Resume the queue and position when the app reopens
- Play counts, **Top** and **History** filters, and listening stats
- Sleep timer option: end of this song
- Backup and restore of favorites, playlists, edits, stats and settings
- Share a song, and "More from this artist / album" shortcuts
- "What's new" note after updating
- GitHub link and update controls in Settings

**Also**
- Apple Lossless (ALAC) `.m4a` playback, Spotify-style notification buttons (favorite and shuffle), and live notification updates when you rename a song or change its cover were added in the 1.0 preview builds and ship with 1.1

## 1.0

- First release: local library, liquid-glass interface, playlists, favorites, queue, themes, rename and change cover
